# LRPD ALPR and RTCC report summary

ARAP analysis; all figures attributed to the source records.

| Report | Scans stated | Alerts stated | Source and qualification |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| First-half 2025 | 111,481,824 | 318,215 | LR-026 p.1; printed June 31; outcome chart extends through December |
| Second-half 2025 | 111,481,824 | 487,949 | LR-027 p.1; printed December 30; December 31 coverage unknown |
| January-June 2026 | 103,375,512 | 350,211 | LR-008/LR-023 p.1; byte-identical copies |

These are numbers stated by LRPD, not independent measurements. Scans are not necessarily unique vehicles, unique plates, or searches. Do not add the two identical 2025 scan figures into a validated annual total.

LR-023 p.2 shows 79,996 official hotlist alerts, 223,952 “Your Custom Hotlist Alerts,” and 46,263 “Other Custom Hotlist Alerts on Your Networks”; these sum to 350,211. The two custom categories sum to 270,215, an ARAP arithmetic calculation of displayed alert categories. This is not a count of custom lists, searches, unique people, unauthorized alerts or misconduct. The categories do not establish the factual purpose of each list or alert.

LR-023 p.3 shows 91 total successes, 86 cases cleared, 54 recovered stolen vehicles and $856,299 recovered property. The report describes outcomes differently from the dashboard labels; the three per-list counts described in the policy are not clearly traceable through these charts. LR-026 p.3 includes January-December 2025 bars even though the report’s first page purports to cover the first half. ARAP asked about these issues in its August 20 email (LR-022); no answer is in the supplied folders.

Operational RTCC metrics include multiple technologies and activities. March/April/May dashboards report 11/11/19 successes (LR-003/004/006 p.2); June reports 16 (LR-018 p.6); Q2 reports 47 (LR-018 p.3). The RTCC semiannual dashboard reports 90 successes (LR-017 p.2), while the separate six-month ALPR report reports 91. These may reflect different snapshots or definitions. Do not treat RTCC arrests, selected success narratives or dashboard successes as counts of prosecutions.
