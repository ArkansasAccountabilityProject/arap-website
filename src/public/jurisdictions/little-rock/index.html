Little Rock ALPR Reports and Q2 Audit Evaluation

The review compares monthly, quarterly, semiannual, statutory, and audit records with General Order 325. The records report very high system volume, but totals do not fully reconcile across the released documents.

The one-page audit conclusion does not disclose the query population, sample, selection method, exception list, or workpapers needed for independent replication. Published reporting also does not identify the statutory non-correlation and prosecution totals.

Limit. A vendor “success” label is not necessarily an arrest, prosecution, conviction, or independently verified outcome.
