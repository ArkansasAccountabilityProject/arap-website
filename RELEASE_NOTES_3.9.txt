ARAP Website Version 3.9 - August 19, 2026

This release adds two complementary public papers while preserving all Version 3.8 material.

Audit vs. Accountability
- Distinguishes after-the-fact audit logs from preventive documentation, authorization, scope control, verification, reporting, and corrective action.
- Uses Arkansas law and selected Sherwood/Cabot records with explicit limits against treating blank reasons, broad network scope, or reporting gaps as automatic proof of misuse.
- Labels municipal approval structures as policy recommendations rather than universal current statutory requirements.
- Treats the search-warrant comparison as a timing/process analogy, not a legal conclusion that every ALPR database query requires a warrant.

Fixed ALPR Camera Geometry and Multi-Image Data
- Publishes the documented approximately 12-foot pole height and 24.8-foot pavement-edge setback used in the technical analysis.
- Derives an approximately 27.6-foot minimum slant distance to the pavement edge using the Pythagorean theorem.
- Makes clear that 27.6 feet is not an exact camera-to-vehicle measurement.
- Declines exact screenshot-derived vehicle distances without focal length, sensor dimensions, camera orientation, distortion calibration, or equivalent site-specific optical evidence.
- Labels Flock capture-distance, speed, lane-coverage, and Vehicle Signature statements as manufacturer-described capabilities.
- Explains that multiple discrete ALPR observations can support retrospective movement inferences without becoming continuous GPS tracking.

Integration and preservation
- Adds both papers to the home page, Publications index, Evidence Repository, Sherwood page, travel-pattern explorer, and audit-evaluation collection.
- Adds four SVG publication figures and checksum files.
- Updates sitemap, README, changelog, package version, release notes, and deployment checklist.
- Preserves all Version 3.8 pages, public-safe screenshots, legal documents, redacted analyses, and downloads.
