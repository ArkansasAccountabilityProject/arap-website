# Third party reporting and Dishman evidence boundaries

No primary agency/CLEST record in this package establishes a Dishman-specific ALPR finding, termination reason, decertification request or final decertification outcome. The October 7 FOIA request (LR-030) seeks underlying misuse and disciplinary records and expressly treats its reported 28-search description as a means of identifying records.

Russ/Snarky Media screenshots were reviewed only as contextual leads and are excluded from the evidence folder, archive and website pages at the user’s direction. Relative social-media labels such as “1d,” “2d,” or “Wed” cannot establish exact publication dates. Reproduced or cropped documents inside social posts are not authenticated originals. Health information, dates of birth, addresses, witnesses and unrelated allegations must not be carried into the ALPR library.

Any later Dishman record should retain its agency/CLEST custodian, request number, original delivery evidence, exact hash, proceeding stage and finality status. Keep an agency request for decertification distinct from a final commission decision. Keep the asserted reason for termination distinct from the alleged ALPR conduct. No identity linkage should be inferred solely from social commentary. No first-to-publish or first-to-obtain claim is supported by this package.
