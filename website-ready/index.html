What the records support

What remains unresolved

Original delivery notifications, the earlier policy, underlying audit logs, clarification responses, and authoritative records about any named officer’s disciplinary or certification outcome are absent from the reviewed evidence. This library does not establish misconduct from alert totals or a reported number of searches.

Primary records and ARAP interpretations are identified separately. Original record downloads require redaction review and approval.
