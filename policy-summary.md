# LRPD General Order 325 summary

ARAP analysis of the July 8 policy copy.

The July 8, 2026 General Order 325 explicitly permits authorized users to create custom hotlists (IV.A.4, p.2). It specifies names containing the user’s full name and rank, one-hotlist guidance, written supervisor approval for additional lists, and monthly audits that purge lists outside the naming convention. Manual entries must be removed when investigative relevance or legitimate law-enforcement interest ends (IV.B.4.c, p.2).

The policy limits server access to criminal-justice reasons by authorized employees, requires an apparent search reason where the query page has a space, and calls for quarterly audits (IV.D.2-4, p.3). General server retention is no longer than 150 days, with specified case-file preservation exceptions (IV.D.1, p.3). Visually reviewed revision marks change monthly reporting to biannual reporting. The policy describes January-June and July-December reports, 18-month preservation, scan totals, list names and three match-outcome counts (IV.D.6-8, pp.3-4). These are summaries of this policy’s text, not conclusions about statutory compliance.

The copy replaces a July 14, 2025 version. That earlier policy is absent. The July 3 Q2 audit predates the revised policy; this library cannot establish which custom-list rules applied to every earlier search.
