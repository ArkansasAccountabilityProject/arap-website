ARAP Website Version 3.7 - August 17, 2026

This release adds two Sherwood source records concerning ALPR reporting and recordkeeping responsibilities.

H-084 preserves the March 13, 2026 internal email in which Administrative Division Captain Scott Hicks sent Arkansas Code sections 12-12-1801 through 12-12-1808 and a draft of Policy 11.08.00 to Chief Jeff Hagar and two other recipients. The attached draft assigned the six-month statistical report to the Chief of Police or designee and required the reports to be retained for eighteen months.

H-085 preserves the August 13, 2026 CITY-2026-140 response stating that statistical data before July 14 could not be provided and that the request identified deficiencies in reporting and recordkeeping procedures. Requester contact details are permanently redacted in the public copy.

The public presentation is centered on documentation and assigned responsibility, not a conclusion for or against ALPR technology or its vendor. It asks who should be held responsible, and at what level of city government, while making clear that the records alone do not establish review of the March attachments, intent, individual fault, legal liability, or the effectiveness of later corrective action.
