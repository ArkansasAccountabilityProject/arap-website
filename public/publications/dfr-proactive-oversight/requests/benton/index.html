Pursuant to the Arkansas Freedom of Information Act, I request electronic copies of the following existing public records concerning the Benton Police Department’s Drone as First Responder (DFR) program involving BRINC Drones and/or Motorola Solutions. Date range: January 1, 2026 through the date this request is processed. Contracts, agreements and trial documents: All contracts, agreements, memoranda of understanding, trial agreements, pilot agreements, demonstration agreements, quotes, proposals, statements of work, order forms, subscription agreements, amendments, addenda, or similar documents between or involving the City of Benton/Benton Police Department and BRINC Drones, Motorola Solutions, or their representatives concerning the DFR program. This specifically includes the record or records identified as “Police Brinc Drone First Responder contract” on the September 15, 2026 Benton Finance Committee agenda. Cost and financial records: Records showing the cost or proposed cost of the DFR program, including equipment, software, subscriptions, installation, infrastructure, communications/networking, maintenance, training, licensing, and any other associated costs. Please also provide records identifying any equipment, services, installation, training, or other consideration being provided at no charge or at a reduced cost during the announced one-year trial, and any proposed costs or contractual obligations that would apply if the City continues the program after the trial. Communications and presentations:

Communications between Benton officials/employees and BRINC Drones and/or Motorola Solutions concerning the proposed DFR program, including emails and attachments discussing the trial, demonstration, procurement, capabilities, implementation, costs, or proposed deployment. Please also provide presentations, talking papers, briefing documents, staff reports, or other materials presented to or prepared for the Mayor, City Council, Finance Committee, Police Department, Fire Department, or other City officials concerning the program. Policies and operating procedures: Current or proposed policies, SOPs, general orders, training materials, operational guidelines, or other written rules governing the DFR program, including records addressing: circumstances permitting a drone launch; who may authorize or initiate a launch; live viewing or monitoring; recording and retention of video or other data; access controls and audit logging; use over private property; automated or computer-generated dispatch/launches; and supervisory review or auditing of drone operations. System integrations: Records describing any current or proposed integration of the DFR system with: Flock Safety or other ALPR systems; license-plate or vehicle alerts/hotlists; Motorola CommandCentral or other Motorola platforms; CAD/911 systems; real-time crime center systems; automated alerts or automated drone-launch functions; or other surveillance, camera, sensor, or public-safety databases. Equipment and deployment: Records identifying the number and model of drones, docking stations, launch locations, cameras/sensors, software platforms, or other equipment being provided, tested, installed, or proposed as part of the trial. FAA authorization: Any FAA waiver, authorization, certificate, application, correspondence, or other record concerning Beyond Visual Line of Sight (BVLOS) operation or other FAA authorization associated with Benton's DFR program. September 15 and September 28 proceedings: Records provided to, considered by, or created for the September 15, 2026 Finance Committee discussion of the “Police Brinc Drone First Responder contract,” and records provided to, considered by, or created for Chief Hodges' “Drone Program Report” appearing on the September 28, 2026 City Council agenda. I request these records electronically in their native electronic format where readily available, including attachments to responsive emails.

If any portion of a responsive record is exempt from disclosure, please redact only the exempt portion and provide the remainder. For each withholding or redaction, please identify the specific statutory basis relied upon. If no responsive records exist for any individual category above, please simply indicate that no responsive records were located for that category. If fulfilling this request will involve fees, please provide an estimate before incurring any charge. Thank you.

Source: FOIA Request.pdf, pp. 1–3, supplied in the Benton production. Verified against the supplied request; review date October 13, 2026.
