Preventive oversight • October 13, 2026

Investigating drone programs before problems arise.

Benton and Sherwood are using Drone as First Responder technology. ARAP is documenting the rules, costs, records and access practices while these programs develop.

We do not need to wait for an accident, privacy complaint or contract dispute to ask how a program is governed. Our aim is to identify what the records establish, what was requested, what was received, and what still needs an answer.

OUR RECORDS PROCESS
  1. Request the existing records.
  2. Preserve and review the production.
  3. Compare it with the request.
  4. Publish supported findings and open questions.
  5. Track additional records and responses.

Questions that should be answered early

Authorization

Who may launch, approve and supervise a flight? How are emergencies, exceptions and FAA conditions documented?

Privacy and retention

What is recorded, how long is it kept, and what governs viewing, sharing, deletion and evidence transfer?

Costs and commitments

Who pays for the infrastructure? What agreement is actually executed? What happens when a trial ends?

Results and review

What do existing logs show about missions, response times, safety events, outcomes and supervisory review?

A dated record of an ongoing investigation

The original Benton request sought program, contract, policy, integration and implementation documents. Additional event-level flight/access questions are separately identified as follow-up work. Sherwood’s October 7 request already expressly sought launch, telemetry, CAD, audit, recording-metadata, safety and outcome records.

“Completed” in Sherwood’s portal is an agency status label. It is not our conclusion that every category is fully answered. “Not identified” means not identified in the reviewed production; it does not establish nonexistence or unlawful withholding.

This is an ongoing records investigation. The records reviewed do not establish misconduct, unauthorized viewing, or improper surveillance. Questions below seek the records necessary to assess the program’s rules, costs, access and outcomes.
