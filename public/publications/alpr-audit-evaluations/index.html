Central Arkansas ALPR Audit Evaluations

Five jurisdiction evaluations compare written controls, released audit records, statistical reporting, outcome documentation, data-sharing evidence, and the limits of what each record set can establish.

How to read this work

Each evaluation separates a verified record observation from an inference or an unresolved question. A missing item means it was not identified in the selected records reviewed; it does not prove that the item does not exist. A network count identifies the scope selected for a query, not the number of agencies that returned or used a result.

Publication limit. The public site provides aggregate evaluations and integrity references. Raw audit records containing names, email addresses, plates, query details, or operational identifiers are not republished here merely because they were produced under a public-records request.

Jurisdiction evaluations

AUD-2026-001

Sherwood

Reviews 1,502 displayed user-audit IDs for May through July 6, three network-inclusion exports, a July 11–August 9 public-portal analysis, and Policy 11.08.00.

Primary review issue: broad network selection and incomplete released evidence showing whether individual searches were independently tied to an incident, case, or other authorization record.

AUD-2026-002

Cabot

Reviews two six-month reports, the 2025 supporting workbook, a 469-row public search-audit export, and Policy 305.1.

Primary review issue: the 2025 memo and workbook do not reconcile on scan totals or records labeled as non-correlating matches.

Download PDF

AUD-2026-003

Jacksonville

Reviews a 267-ID audit PDF, a 349-organization participation list, and Jacksonville's ALPR policy.

Primary review issue: the released PDF is useful for aggregate review but its flattened format and incomplete visible case field limit reliable case-level reconciliation.

Download PDF

AUD-2026-004

Little Rock

Reviews monthly, quarterly, semiannual, and audit materials alongside General Order 325.

Primary review issue: published totals conflict across reports, and the one-page audit conclusion does not disclose the population, sample, method, or workpapers needed for independent replication.

Download PDF

AUD-2026-005

Pulaski County

Reviews two overlapping short-period reports and materially inconsistent 2021 and 2025 policies.

Primary review issue: the 2025 document repeatedly applies Virginia law to an Arkansas sheriff's office, while the supplied reports are not six-month reports and expressly say outcomes were not tracked.
