What supports the Mayor’s 40% Flock case-solving claim?

Watch the debate excerpt, read ARAP’s original records request, and examine what Sherwood supplied. The six-file production does not substantiate the percentage. It does not establish that the percentage is false.

Finding · Video · Original request · Six documents · Requested vs. supplied · Provenance

The city’s written response

“We do not have any supporting documentation to substantiate the statement that 40 percent of our cases are solved through the use of Flock license plate reader technology.”

Captain Scott Hicks, October 20, 2026, 3:32 PM. CITY 2026-193.pdf, page 1 (public redacted copy).

ARAP asked the City for existing records supporting the claim, including the reporting period, numerator, denominator, definitions of “case” and “solved,” and how an outcome was attributed to Flock. No such calculation or underlying counts appear in this production. Without them, the percentage cannot be reproduced from these records.

Scope of the conclusion. This response establishes a documentation gap, not the true case-solving rate, dishonesty, intent, or whether Flock ever assists investigations. Asking the City for records is different from obtaining a personal explanation from the Mayor. This production does not identify her own source, nor establish that she was personally asked for an explanation or received this request.

**Subject: Arkansas FOIA Request — Flock Audit Procedures, Data Retention, Corrective Actions, and Supporting Records** Dear Records Custodian: I am an Arkansas citizen requesting electronic copies of existing public records under the Arkansas Freedom of Information Act, Ark. Code Ann. §§25-19-101 et seq. This request follows CITY-2026-140 and the October 13, 2026 Sherwood mayoral debate. During that debate, Mayor Mary Jo Heye-Townsell described monthly Flock audits by Captain Scott Hicks, automatic deletion after 30 days, “in-house” storage, required login information, and a finding that 40% of cases are solved using Flock. The purpose is to identify the records documenting these practices and any changes made after the reporting and recordkeeping deficiencies identified in the City’s August 13, 2026 response. This request does not assume that the practices are new or that any statement was inaccurate. **Date range:** January 1, 2026, through the date the City receives this request. Please also provide any earlier policy, procedure, assignment, agreement, or configuration record still governing the practices described below. **1. Audit procedures and assignments** Please provide all existing policies, procedures, directives, memoranda, checklists, templates, and instructions governing Flock/ALPR audits or supervisory reviews, including: - Audit frequency, scope, sampling methods, and documentation requirements. - Assignment of audit duties to Captain Hicks or other personnel. - Adoption, approval, effective dates, and revisions. - Instructions for checking search purposes, case numbers, authorized access, retention, sharing, and prohibited uses. **2. Completed audits and supervisory reviews** Please provide each completed monthly, periodic, or annual Flock/ALPR audit or compliance review during the requested period, including reports, completed checklists, workpapers, review logs, sign-offs, findings, and documented corrective actions. This includes existing records identifying the review date, reviewer, period examined, records sampled, exceptions found, and disposition of those exceptions. This category seeks documentation of reviews actually performed, in addition to underlying system-generated activity logs. **3. Retention, deletion, and preservation** Please provide existing records documenting: - The retention settings governing plate images, vehicle images, search results, alert/outcome records, statistical data, and audit logs. - Automatic deletion schedules and records of changes to those settings. - Exceptions for investigations, prosecutions, legal holds, or other preservation. - Procedures for downloading, exporting, or archiving information outside the Flock platform. - Existing export/ archive logs, inventories, schedules, or completion records. - Retention and deletion rules applying to downloaded or archived copies. Please include records distinguishing routine platform deletion from preservation of copies elsewhere. **4. Storage and custody** Please provide existing agreements, service descriptions, policies, inventories, diagrams, or correspondence identifying where Flock/ALPR records are stored and which entity hosts, administers, possesses, or controls them. Include records distinguishing vendor-hosted storage from City-maintained storage or archives, and identifying any vendor or subcontractor with storage or processing responsibilities. I do not request passwords, encryption keys, credentials, network addresses, or other security-sensitive technical details. **5. Login and search-documentation requirements** Please provide existing non-security- sensitive policies, user instructions, forms, field descriptions, or configuration records documenting requirements for: - Individual user identification or badge numbers. - Case or incident numbers. - Search reasons or investigative justifications. - Whether each requirement applies at login, at search, or when recording an alert outcome. - Review or handling of missing, incomplete, or nonspecific entries. Actual passwords, access tokens, and authentication secrets are excluded. **6. Corrective actions following CITY-2026-140** Please provide records documenting the review and revision of reporting or recordkeeping procedures described in the August 13, 2026 response, including recommendations, revised procedures, implementation dates, approvals, training notices, vendor correspondence, and existing records evaluating whether corrective actions were completed. Please include completed six-month statistical reports prepared during the requested period under Policy 11.08.00. **7. Records supporting the 40% assertion** Please provide existing reports, spreadsheets, dashboards, calculations, summaries, memoranda, or other records supporting the statement that 40% of cases are solved using Flock. Include existing records showing the reporting period, numerator, denominator, definition of “case” and “solved,” and method used to attribute outcomes to Flock. This category does not ask the City to create a calculation or conduct a new analysis. **8. Supporting correspondence** Please provide communications transmitting, approving, revising, or discussing the records and practices requested above, including communications among the Police Department, Mayor’s Office, FOIA personnel, Information Technology Department, and Flock representatives. **Format and response** Please provide electronic records in their existing native or ordinary export format when readily available, including CSV, XLSX, PDF, and complete email chains with responsive attachments. To avoid duplicate production, an exact identification and accessible link to a previously produced record is acceptable. Please provide revised versions and records not previously produced. I do not seek captured plate images, private individuals’ license plate numbers, or identifying details of subjects of investigations. Please redact exempt information and release reasonably segregable nonexempt portions. For each numbered category, please identify whether responsive records are provided, no responsive records exist, records are withheld under an identified exemption, or another custodian has administrative control. If another custodian is known or readily ascertainable, please identify that custodian. Please preserve responsive records that would otherwise be deleted or overwritten while this request is pending. Rolling production is requested as records become available. Please acknowledge receipt and respond within the applicable statutory time requirements. Thank you, John D. McKeehan Arkansas Accountability Project

Response instructions also matter

The request asked for native or ordinary export formats, complete chains/attachments, accessible identification of earlier productions if used in place of duplicates, a status for each category (provided, nonexistent, withheld with exemption, or another custodian), preservation and rolling production. The inspected ZIP contains six PDFs, not a category-by-category disposition or native statistical dataset. The discipline email cites a disclosure provision for that subject; it is not a blanket disposition of all eight categories. The drone memo lists an attachment but this ZIP does not identify a corresponding attached contract as part of that email. No reason for the drone documents’ inclusion is provided.

What would allow the public to test 40%?

A defined reporting period, total eligible cases, count solved using a stated attribution rule, definitions of case and solved, and supporting records permitting reconciliation without exposing investigative subjects. Any later correction, explanation or supplemental production should be dated and linked here alongside this record.
