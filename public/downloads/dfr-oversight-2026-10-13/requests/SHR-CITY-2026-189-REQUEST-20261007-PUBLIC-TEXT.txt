Pursuant to the Arkansas Freedom of Information Act, I request electronic copies of the following existing public records concerning the Sherwood Police Department’s BRINC/Motorola Drone as First Responder (DFR) program. Date range: From the earliest available record of any launch, flight, deployment, test, training exercise, demonstration, or operational use of the installed Sherwood DFR system through the date this request is received. Please provide the following records: Flight and mission logs for each DFR drone launch or attempted launch during the responsive period, including any available fields reflecting the date; launch and landing/recovery times; flight duration; mission or event type; incident, CAD, call-for-service, or case number; launch location or dock; response location or destination; pilot, operator, teleoperator, or user identifier; reason for deployment; and mission disposition or outcome. BRINC Responder, LiveOps, or other system-generated activity records documenting launches, deployments, missions, attempted or aborted launches, cancellations, returns, and completed flights. CAD or dispatch records associated with DFR deployments, including records showing the call, incident, or event associated with a drone response and any existing field or record indicating whether the deployment was automatically generated, dispatcher initiated, officer requested, pilot initiated, or initiated by another method. Records showing who initiated, authorized, approved, piloted, supervised, or remotely assisted a DFR flight, to the extent that information is recorded by BRINC, LiveOps, CAD, dispatch, or another system. Flight-history and telemetry records, including available system-generated exports reflecting flight paths, altitude, distance, duration, launch and landing locations or coordinates, or comparable flight-history information. Records or metadata showing whether video or still imagery was captured during a mission and, where maintained as existing metadata, whether the recording was retained, deleted, uploaded or transferred to an evidence-management system, associated with an incident or case number, livestreamed, or otherwise made available to personnel during the flight. At this time, this request seeks records and metadata concerning recordings, not copies of the underlying video or imagery itself. Audit or activity logs reflecting access to DFR missions, live video, recorded video, flight histories, or mission records, including available fields identifying the user, timestamp, action performed, and type of access. Records documenting failed or aborted launches, emergency landings, loss of communications, GPS or navigation failures, geofence events, airspace warnings, Remote ID issues, collision or near-collision events, or other documented flight-related exceptions or safety events. Records documenting any instance in which a DFR flight was restricted, delayed, cancelled, rerouted, prevented, or otherwise affected because of FAA authorization requirements, controlled airspace, LAANC, BVLOS limitations or authorization, weather, proximity to an airport or airfield, Temporary Flight Restrictions, or another aviation or airspace restriction. Any existing daily, weekly, monthly, cumulative, or system-generated reports or dashboards reflecting DFR activity or outcomes, including number of deployments or launches, flights, flight hours, response times, calls responded to, cancellations, arrests, apprehensions, searches, missing-person incidents, or other outcomes attributed to the DFR program. Electronic/native format For records maintained electronically or generated by BRINC, Motorola, LiveOps, CAD, dispatch, or another computerized system, I request the records in their native or ordinary system-exported electronic format where readily available, including CSV, XLSX, JSON, PDF, or another standard export format, rather than screenshots or individually printed records. I am not requesting that the City create a new record, report, compilation, or analysis that does not already exist. Rolling or automatically deleted records This request includes responsive records existing as of the City's receipt of this request, including records maintained under rolling, automatic, or scheduled deletion or retention periods. Please take reasonable steps to preserve responsive records that would otherwise be automatically deleted, overwritten, or become unavailable while this request is being processed. This includes responsive BRINC Responder/LiveOps flight, mission, deployment, telemetry, audit, CAD-integration, video-metadata, and system-activity records. If responsive records are subject to a rolling or automatic retention period, please identify the applicable retention period and the earliest date for which responsive records remained available when this request was received. Rolling / incremental production requested If responsive records are located, reviewed, and available for release at different times, please provide responsive records on a rolling basis as they become available rather than withholding records that are ready for production until all potentially responsive records have been located, reviewed, or processed. For example, if flight or mission logs are available before related CAD records, telemetry, audit logs, or other responsive records, please provide the available records first and supplement the production as the remaining responsive records become available. Redactions or withheld records If any responsive record contains information determined to be exempt from disclosure, please redact only the exempt portion and provide the reasonably segregable remainder. For any record withheld in whole or in part, please identify the specific statutory basis for the withholding or redaction. If no responsive records exist for a numbered category above, please indicate that no responsive records were located for that category. Electronic production is preferred. Thank you.
