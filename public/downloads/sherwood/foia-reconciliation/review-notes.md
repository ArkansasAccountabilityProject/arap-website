## Meaning of labels

Relevant records identified: responsive material located, not a certification of full compliance.
Partial / reconciliation needed: some relevant records exist but scope, versions, dates or completeness remain unresolved.
Not identified: collection-level observation, not a statement of nonexistence or withholding.
City reports unavailable / no supporting documentation: limited to the exact response/category.
Pending City clarification: latest preserved correspondence has not resolved the question.
Related records in another request: cross-request lead requiring identity, scope and delivery reconciliation.
Format / attachment verification: native/embedded/named attachments need exact matching.
Disposition unverified: archive does not establish processing outcome.
New request / response not assessed: no overdue or denial determination.

## Source and privacy handling

All original sources remain unchanged. The public source index identifies filenames, pages and original SHA-256 fingerprints; selected quotations are transcriptions. Only reviewed public copies are offered for download. Other originals remain in ARAP's archive. Requester contacts/home details, portal security keys and identifying plate/vehicle/movement records are not in the public dataset. The original ZIP download date is not presumed receipt, first availability or completion. ZIP copies/extracted folders are not separate productions.

Underlying inventory: 963 physical files with OCR/text search and 15 automatic video transcripts. CITY-103 original confirmation and corrected completion additionally verified through connected Gmail. This does not certify every attachment, CSV row or custodian search. CITY-165/166 processing remains an explicit verification task. The register distinguishes requested categories from later investigative follow-ups.
