# CITY-2026-198: procurement production and effect on earlier findings

Arkansas Accountability Project | Reviewed October 23, 2026

## Principal finding

The supplied archive contains **68 files**, including **60 PDFs totaling 570 pages**, **five MSG files**, **two DOCX files**, and **one TXT file**. SHA-256 comparisons establish that **67 files are exact copies of records in the selected earlier productions**: all 58 members of the October 5-labelled CITY-2026-150 ZIP and all nine members of the CITY-2026-164 ZIP. The one file not found in that selected prior set is **AAP FOIA Capt edwards.pdf**, a three-page email thread. [A1; comparison-inventory.csv]

The new thread is consequential despite the overlap. It documents the FOIA Coordinator's broader routing of ARAP's October 21 clarification and Capt. Drew Edwards's response about TIPS and the Takeoff Program. It changes the earlier statement that no substantive City answer to that clarification had been identified. It does **not** establish completed searches by all recipients, legal review before signing, the applicable cooperative contract and award terms, or a complete seven-category disposition of CITY-2026-198. [N1 pp. 1-3]

Repeated records are not automatically nonresponsive or an improper response. CITY-2026-198 expressly allowed exact identification and accessible links for previously produced records. Its categories overlap heavily with CITY-2026-150/164. The relevant question is whether the supplied records and explanations address the procurement request, not how many megabytes or previously unseen filenames were delivered. [R1 pp. 1-4]

## What CITY-2026-198 actually requested

The request concerns **procurement, authorization, financing and contract execution**, expressly distinguishing its subject from operational flight records. It seeks seven categories:

1. Complete executed agreement, incorporated documents and contractual obligations.
2. Existing contract-value and financial-evaluation records.
3. Competitive bidding and procurement authority, including any relied-upon exemptions or cooperative purchasing documentation.
4. Council and administrative approval, routing, signature authority and nonexempt guidance.
5. Free-trial, cancellation and related representations.
6. Installation, integration and additional costs and their authorizations.
7. Communications materially concerning procurement, execution, purchasing approval or financial obligations.

The date range begins January 1, 2025 and ends at receipt, together with earlier incorporated/relied-upon procurement records. The portal shows submission October 20 but not the actual receipt hour or timezone. It requests existing records, accepts exact identification and accessible links for prior productions, asks the City to coordinate custodians and provide each numbered category's disposition, and expressly disclaims a requirement to create a new legal analysis, financial calculation or written explanation. [R1 pp. 1-4]

The statement in the request about a six-year arrangement and approximately $79,999 annually for five years is the requester's framing. It is not independently transformed here into a finding of the City's legally committed contract value, bidding threshold or procurement classification.

### October 21, 1:30 PM: Capt. Edwards response

Edwards responds internally to James and other officials. His message makes two substantive points. [N1 p. 1]

**TIPS:** He says Perez forwarded an email confirming that staff checked with BRINC about TIPS, that Edwards also searched the TIPS website, that BRINC's DFR service is listed there, and that Sherwood appears to have been a member since 2013. He says vendor contract documents are on the TIPS website and “We do not maintain or keep any of those records.”

These are now documented City statements. The selected production does not, through this message alone, establish the particular cooperative contract number, vendor/reseller award, covered services, applicable term, transaction authorization, or the version relied upon at execution. No independently verified TIPS membership/listing conclusion is made here. No separately identified new vendor-confirmation email accompanies N1; the City should identify the exact existing file/page/message referenced if it is within the reused material or supplied elsewhere. A keyword/OCR search of the prior merged extracts did not identify an explicit cooperative-purchasing confirmation, but is not proof that none exists or that no obscured or unrecognized passage addresses it.

The non-maintenance statement is specifically about those TIPS vendor-contract records. It is not a category-wide certification that the City has no procurement, approval, financial, legal-review or vendor-correspondence records. It does not by itself establish the absence of administrative control or resolve every original category. The message gives a general website reference, not an exact accessible record link or contract identifier.

**No-cost first year:** Edwards describes his stance that no drone equipment or BRINC services have been purchased, while acknowledging a signed BRINC/Motorola Takeoff agreement that allows one year of equipment/service use from go-live without cost, with termination for convenience during that year. He says this appears in the contract and the documents were already provided.

This documents the department's interpretation and points to already produced contract material. It does not, by itself, decide whether the arrangement is a procurement under Arkansas law, whether future obligations require approval or bidding, whether the entire incorporated contract was supplied, or whether separate infrastructure costs were incurred. The signed Products Agreement and associated quote/service language must be read together with their incorporated terms and any applicable amendments. A no-subscription-cost period does not establish zero installation, electrical, network or integration costs.

N1 contains no Sourcewell-specific substantive answer from the City. Sourcewell appears in the reproduced requester email. No City Attorney, Municipal League or council approval record is established simply because officials were recipients of this thread.

## Format, redactions, scope and response completeness

The file mix includes five native MSGs and two DOCX files, so this cannot accurately be characterized as an all-scanned-PDF production. Many important compilations and contracts remain image-based PDFs. Ordinary/native format was requested when reasonably available; the availability of additional native exports or conversion constraints is not established. [R1 p. 3; A1 inventory]

All 67 carried-forward records are byte-identical to the earlier selected files. That means the earlier redactions, black-rendering pages and attachment limits persist in those copies. It is not a fresh count of newly imposed redactions. In particular, five carried-forward quote/proposal files retain the previously documented black-rendering pages 7-10, and the same earlier versions of the CITY-164 email compilation/credentials are present. The new email contains no field-specific statutory exemption explanation for those redactions. Earlier explanations, separate correspondence or later supplements may exist outside this selected record set.

The request explicitly asked for a produced/no-records/withheld disposition for each numbered category. N1 offers a substantive TIPS non-maintenance statement and contract interpretation, but is not a complete seven-category disposition. It does not say that every requested category has no further records or that the City's search is complete. The preserved request-details PDF shows the initial Submitted state, not the final status; no separate later completion notice or cover response is in this folder.

No adverse deadline conclusion is made. The administrative thread's reference to three in-progress requests does not identify them; the earlier staff claim of a timing waiver in the CITY-164 routing email is not independently verified by this production. No newly created legal analysis, financial calculation, full search narrative or record-by-record withholding index is assumed legally required.

4. **Reconcile earlier cross-request gaps.** Records previously identified only in CITY-150/164 are now physically included together under CITY-198. An earlier observation about the contents of the nine-file CITY-164 ZIP remains historically true. It must not be turned into a present claim that those same records were never supplied.
5. **Do not change unrelated findings without evidence.** A1 adds no new CITY-189 flight/audit exports and does not resolve CITY-112's disclosure-handling questions. It does not by itself supply the actual COA or Class Instructions attachment as attached to the CITY-164 parent email, even though previously produced FAA material is included elsewhere.
6. **Do not describe this as 170 MB of new evidence.** It is 67 exact prior file copies plus one new-to-this-selected-set three-page thread. That thread should be assessed on its content, not dismissed because the rest repeats earlier records.

## Method and limits

Every archive member was inventoried, SHA-256 hashed and compared with the exact prior selected ZIP-member inventory. PDF page counts were read from all 60 PDF page trees. The three-page new file was text-extracted and all three pages visually inspected. Its statements were separated from the quoted requester email and from independent conclusions. The request PDF was extracted in full and its physical page locators retained. Earlier file analyses were reused only where the exact bytes matched; these 67 files were not represented as newly re-read line by line. The prior OCR keyword search is a search aid, not a definitive negative search of City systems.

No raw original ZIP is republished merely because it was produced. Existing native emails contain requester personal fields and other material requiring publication review. Public derivatives and source excerpts are labelled, and the full private inventory must not be copied into a public website. The new thread's visible content was checked for publication: official business contacts and the public ARAP contact appear; no requester street address, personal phone, request access key or vendor access link was identified in its three pages. Its annotation inventory contained official business contact links and an email-security redirect behind the public ARAP homepage link. The public copy removes these link annotations and unreferenced objects while retaining visible content and searchable text. Original and public hashes are distinguished; the original PDF is retained privately.

This review determines what the supplied material establishes. It does not certify the City's search, determine the procurement's legality, establish a financial liability, or infer intentional concealment, destruction or failure to respond from an archive alone.
