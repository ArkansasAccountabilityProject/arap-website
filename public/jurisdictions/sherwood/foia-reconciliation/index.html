How to read this register

ARAP compares preserved requests, City statements, dated ZIP inventories and received records. Category labels summarize substantive scope; they are not verbatim transcripts. “Relevant records identified” means responsive material was located, not that every aspect of a category is certified complete.

  • Corrected and supplemented: later records count. Earlier missing Permit Department correspondence, network exports and finance vendor attachments are not automatically treated as still missing.
  • Cross-request evidence: a record in another release is a lead for reconciliation, not proof the City withheld it intentionally. Exact document identity and scope dates matter.
  • Pending versus unanswered: new CITY-197, 198 and 199 submissions are tracked without an overdue finding. CITY-170’s promised update is pending in the latest reviewed correspondence.
  • Privacy: requester home/contact details, portal keys, raw vehicle images, plate values and identifying movement records are excluded from the public dataset.
  • Coverage: the underlying review inventoried 963 physical files and used text extraction, OCR and video transcripts. CITY-103’s confirmation and corrected completion were additionally verified through connected Gmail. This register does not certify every attachment, spreadsheet row or custodian search. CITY-165/166’s processing relationship remains unresolved.
