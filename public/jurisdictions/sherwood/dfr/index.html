What the four files establish

Mission labels include patrol and security checks

118 of 218 rows (54.1%) are solely Training (79), Test Flight (35) or Maintenance/Test Flight (4). One additional row mixes Civil Assist and Training; six classifications are blank.

Other labels include missing-person searches, disturbances, fire responses, traffic stops, suspicious circumstances, directed patrols and security checks. The labels do not independently establish the incident circumstances or whether a flight was proper.

Source: summary classifications, pp. 4–6

Launch addresses cover three cities

The visible Launch Address field includes Sherwood, North Little Rock and Jacksonville. The separate Location field and case/flight identifiers are redacted.

Limit: launch address is not a route, destination or surveillance footprint. Native telemetry and incident links are needed.

Source: summary, pp. 1–3

The “Flight Log” is a page-access log

562 events: 356 show-page visits, 197 mission-page visits, eight sync-video views and one diagram visit. 28 visits lack exit times and durations.

Page duration is not flight duration or proof of continuous viewing. Overlapping visits should not be summed as elapsed viewing time.

Source: page-access log, pp. 1–10

Sharing and authentication need careful interpretation

12 links: ten expired, two revoked; configured windows range from 30 minutes to eight hours. Recipients and viewers are not identified.

The authentication log contains a recurring Successful login / False followed by Successful 2FA / True pattern. False alone is not a failed login; there are 19 explicitly unsuccessful login events and ten failed-2FA events.

ShareLink workbook · Authentication example, p. 3

Method and limits

Summary pages 1–3 contain flight fields; pages 4–6 contain the corresponding Call Type column and are matched by row position. Airtime is approximate because the summary is scanned. Authentication/page events are counted by distinct visible IDs. Link windows are calculated from creation/expiration fields.

Redacted identifiers prevent reliable individual flight-to-case-to-share matching. Other exports need timezone confirmation before exact matching with the summary’s CDT / UTC−5 timestamps.
