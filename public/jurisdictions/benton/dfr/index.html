Documented findings

The agreement changed on paper

The original three-site, six-year quote totals $1,476,615.78 including estimated tax. The later Trial Service Agreement, signed on the City side September 28, would replace the December 2, 2025 agreement with a 12-month trial and negotiated continuation subject to City Council approval.

Limit: Motorola’s signature is absent from the supplied amendment. September 30 emails request its signature. The operative agreement and exact trial dates remain unverified.

Sources: BentonPD_BRINC_DFR_101325 – Signed.pdf, pp. 2–3; BNPD BRINC AMENDED AGREEMENT.pdf, p. 1; Sept. 30 correspondence.

Infrastructure was separately invoiced

Electrical work $9,438.18; fiber/networking $3,624.25; pad/fencing $7,528.00; CAD interface $1,250.00. Total: $21,840.43.

Limit: These are four invoices billed to Benton PD. Payment, reimbursement and full-program costs are not established. Crane correspondence says BRINC would pay for crane service.

Sources: Brad Glover #8079; Ultimate Communications #15809; Bill Wright invoice Sept. 3; Southern Software #26344 / PO 20262069.

Installation and operation are different milestones

Installation records identify PD, Event Center and Fire Station 5, with sign-off completed August 6 locally. September 8–9 certificates document vendor training; a September 17 email says the program went live that week. A September 29 public announcement invited an October 8 demonstration.

The high school and utility location were earlier candidates. They are not established as installed stations by those planning records.

Sources: installation sign-off pp. 1–9; site survey; training certificates; Outlook(9), p. 2; supplied announcement screenshot.

Operating rules and access need reconciliation

The folder includes two Policy 1014 versions, one Proposed, plus a qualifications SOP. Both policy files retain a 2017 effective date; no adoption instrument establishes which version governed at launch.

The sign-off records county geofence, automatic recording/log offload and two faulty batteries awaiting replacement. These settings and notes are not proof of unauthorized flights, viewing or use of faulty batteries.

Sources: policies; SOP 1014-1; installation sign-off pp. 3, 8.

Request date: October 1, 2026. Requested period: January 1, 2026 through processing. The supplied request specifically names the September 15 Finance Committee contract discussion and September 28 City Council Drone Program Report.

Our next records questions

The October 1 request did not expressly request every event-level operational flight and access log. Those additional records are a separate follow-up, not automatically an unanswered category of the original request.

  • Obtain the returned countersigned trial agreement, incorporated terms, commencement date and deadline.
  • Confirm adopted policy, retention/sharing rules and the five-flights-per-60-days currency documentation.
  • Request native flight, telemetry, authentication, page/video access and ShareLink records.
  • Obtain payment/reimbursement records, government-proceeding materials and actual CAD export fields.
  • Resolve the battery replacements and clarify current configuration/public dashboard status.
