Cabot ALPR Audit Evaluation

The review covers Policy 305.1, January–June reports for 2024 and 2025, the 2025 supporting workbook, and a public 469-row search-audit export.

The 2025 memo reports 7,023,170 scans and three false hits. The workbook totals 7,555,734 scans and 617 rows labeled “Matches that Did Not Correlate to the Alert.” The records therefore require reconciliation; the discrepancy is not proof that either figure was intentionally misstated.

Limit. The released public audit masks user identities and does not, by itself, establish whether any query was authorized or unauthorized.
