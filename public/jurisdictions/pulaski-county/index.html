AUD-2026-005 • August 12, 2026

Pulaski County ALPR Audit Evaluation

The review compares a 2021 policy, a 2025 Policy 43, and two overlapping reports. The 2025 policy repeatedly cites Virginia law, Virginia FOIA, and Virginia State Police procedures for an Arkansas sheriff's office and does not state that it supersedes the 2021 policy.

The reports cover 29 and 70 days, overlap from December 29 through January 6, are labeled as six-month reports, and expressly state that outcomes were not tracked. They therefore cannot be added or treated as complete six-month statutory reports.

Limit. These are documentary inconsistencies requiring correction or clarification, not proof of unlawful intent or misuse.
